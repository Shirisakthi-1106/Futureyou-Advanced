## Part 2: Understanding & Testing the 3D Avatar Feature 🔮

### What IS the 3D Avatar?

The avatar is **NOT a character** - it's **animated particles** that react to your predictions.

Think of it like a **visual representation of your mental state**:
- **Good mental state** → Calm, cyan (pale blue) waves
- **Bad mental state** → Chaotic, red/purple waves

### Where Is It?

The avatar is on **EVERY PAGE** - it's the **animated background**.

## Common Issues:

**Q: I don't see any animation in the background**
A: The animation is subtle. Look for:
- Small dots/particles moving
- Gentle wave-like motions
- Color (should be purple-ish by default)
- If you see nothing, refresh the page

**Q: The background doesn't change color**
A: Make sure you:
1. Actually move the sliders to EXTREME values (not just a little)
2. Click "Generate Trajectory" (not just scroll)
3. Wait for the page to load (it takes 2-3 seconds)
4. Look carefully at the background (it's subtle, not bright)

**Q: I see numbers but what do they mean?**
A: 
- **Exam 75** = You'll score about 75/100
- **Stress 45%** = You'll have moderate stress (45% on a scale)
- **Dropout 15%** = 15% chance you'll drop out
- **Wellbeing 8** = Good overall life satisfaction (out of 10)

**Q: Why is the wave sometimes purple?**
A: Because your habits are in the MIDDLE range (not excellent, not terrible). Middle = purple avatar.
